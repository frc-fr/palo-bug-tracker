Data origin

The data is automatically extracted from the PAN-OS Release Notes published by Palo Alto Networks on docs.paloaltonetworks.com. Only the HTML pages are read: the robots.txt file of that site forbids robots on the PDF copies. Each bug links to the page of the official note mentioning it. Support dates of the branches come from the "End-of-Life Summary" page of Palo Alto Networks, and the hardware end of life from its "Hardware End-of-Life Dates" page.
